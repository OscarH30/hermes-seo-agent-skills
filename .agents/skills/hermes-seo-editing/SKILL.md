# Hermes SEO Editing

Improve existing copy through focused passes while preserving its core message, approved voice, and factual boundaries. Editing is not a license to make unsupported marketing claims.

## Boundary

- Obtain the existing copy, page job, audience/intent, voice direction, and proof sources. Where context is missing, make clearly labeled INFERRED choices from the page and site, name the GAPs, and continue unless the missing context makes a revision unsafe or materially misleading. Label source-backed claims OBSERVED.
- Never add invented claims, testimonials, metrics, citations, pricing, locations, certifications, legal assertions, or competitor statements.
- Preserve original meaning unless evidence requires a claim to be softened, removed, or flagged.
- Do not publish or make CMS, metadata, schema, redirect, or external-link changes without explicit human approval.

## Sequential passes

1. Clarity: simplify unclear phrasing, jargon, ambiguity, and overloaded sentences. Keep one main idea per section.
2. Intent and structure: confirm the page answers the searcher’s task, uses meaningful headings, and leads to a useful next action.
3. Evidence and trust: flag unsupported, outdated, vague, absolute, or unattributed statements. Verify citations against their original sources.
4. Voice, authority, and persuasion: retain the established tone, use concrete reader-relevant language, remove empty superlatives, and use a real owner/expert quote when one is available and accurate. If no verified quote exists, strengthen authority through specific evidence and expert framing instead. If a quoted expert has a verified team/author page, propose a contextual internal link; never manufacture quotations, credentials, or affiliation.
5. Sources and SEO usability: verify factual citations against original or official sources. Use Firecrawl MCP or Perplexity MCP for discovery only, then check the underlying source. Improve natural title/H1/headings, direct-answer clarity, internal-link suggestions, and scannability without keyword stuffing.
6. Human-author pass: remove formulaic transitions, repetitive sentence patterns, vague filler, mechanical keyword use, and unearned certainty. Replace them with specific evidence, attributable perspective, natural cadence, and useful editorial judgment. Do not claim AI-detector evasion.
7. Final integrity: confirm the revision did not introduce new claims or change legal/brand meaning.

## Deliverable

Return a concise before/after table: location; concern; proposed edit; reason; evidence or confirmation needed. Then provide a clean revised draft only after the table. Include metadata/internal-link/schema suggestions as non-deployed notes. End with the limited claims to confirm and the external actions requiring explicit human approval before publication.
